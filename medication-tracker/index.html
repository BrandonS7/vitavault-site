Questions

Can VitaVault read my pill bottle label?

Yes. Choose Scan Label and take a photo of the label, or pick one from your photo library. With your consent, the photo is sent through our server to Anthropic Claude, with Google Gemini as a backup, and the name, strength, form, directions, quantity, refills, prescriber, pharmacy and Rx number are filled in for you to check before you save.

Can I add medications without scanning?

Yes. Choose Add Manually and type in whatever you know. Only the name is required. Supplements and vitamins work the same way.

How do medication reminders work?

Turn on reminders for a medication, add one or more times, and pick the days of the week. VitaVault schedules notifications on your iPhone, so they arrive even when the app is closed.

Can I share my medication list with my doctor?

Yes. Doctor Visit Prep makes a report that lists your current medications with strength, form and the AM and PM doses you entered, along with recent lab results.

Is my medication list sent anywhere?

Your medication list is stored on your iPhone, and no account is needed. A label scan asks for your consent first and sends the label photo for that request. We don't keep your health data. If you decline, nothing is sent and you can add the medication by hand.

How much does it cost?

VitaVault is free to download and includes 10 AI queries in your first month. Premium is a one-time $4.99 purchase with 6 months of AI Insights included (50 a month, resets monthly). AI Insights is also available on its own for $2.99 a month.
