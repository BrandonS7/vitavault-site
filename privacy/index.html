Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

Your health data is stored on your iPhone, and no account is needed. If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, your medications, lab results, journal and check-ins also sync through your own iCloud account. When you use AI features or scans, only what that request needs is sent through our server to generate the answer, as described below. We don't keep your health data. The one exception is OpenClaw sync through VitaVault Cloud, which is off unless you turn it on (see OpenClaw Sync below).

Data Collection

VitaVault reads health data from Apple HealthKit with your explicit permission. This data is:

  • Stored on your iPhone
  • Never uploaded or shared, except what an AI feature or scan sends when you use it (see AI Insights below), or what OpenClaw sync sends if you connect it
  • Never used for advertising or analytics
  • Only exported when you explicitly choose to export

AI Insights

AI features run only after you accept the consent prompt. Each request sends our Cloudflare Worker only what that feature needs: your question, and depending on the feature, a summary of your health numbers (such as steps, heart rate, and sleep hours), your medication list, or your lab results. Lab scan sends the text your iPhone read from the report, which can include anything printed on it. Label scan sends the label photo. The worker passes this to Anthropic Claude, with Google Gemini as a backup, to generate the answer, and does not keep it.

To apply monthly AI limits, each AI and scan request also carries a random install ID created by the app and, if you bought Premium or AI Insights, Apple's signed purchase record (product, transaction IDs, and purchase and expiry dates). Neither includes your name, email, or Apple ID. We keep only usage counters keyed to these IDs, and to your IP address for free users. The counters expire automatically.

If you don't use AI features, label scan, lab scan, or OpenClaw sync, no health data leaves your phone.

OpenClaw Sync

OpenClaw sync is optional and off until you connect it in Settings and confirm the consent prompt. It sends your health metrics, medications, and labs to an OpenClaw AI agent that you run, so it can answer questions about them. There are two ways to connect:

  • VitaVault Cloud: your data is stored on our server under a connection key unique to your device, and your agent fetches it with that key. This is the only case where we store health data.
  • Direct webhook: your data goes straight from your iPhone to the web address and token you enter, without passing through our server.

Disconnect any time in Settings to stop syncing. To have data already stored on VitaVault Cloud removed, contact us at the address below.

Medication Data

Medication information you enter (names, dosages, reminders) is stored locally on your device. Photos of medication labels are sent through our Cloudflare Worker to Anthropic Claude, with Google Gemini as a backup, to read the label, and the worker does not keep them.

iCloud Sync

If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, VitaVault syncs your medications, lab results, journal entries and daily check-ins through your own private iCloud account, so they can show up on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple stores this data in your iCloud, not on VitaVault's servers, and we can't see it. VitaVault does not sync your Apple Health readings or export files this way.

Exports

When you export your health data (JSON or CSV), the file is saved on your iPhone, inside the VitaVault app. We never see it.

No Accounts

VitaVault does not require an account, email address, or any personal information to use. There is no sign-up process.

No Tracking

We do not use analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or any form of user behavior monitoring. We don't know who you are or how you use the app.

Third Parties

VitaVault does not sell or share your data for advertising. The only external communication is:

  • AI features and scans, sent through our server to Anthropic Claude, with Google Gemini as a backup, when you use them
  • OpenClaw sync, only if you connect it
  • Apple's App Store for in-app purchases

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time from Settings → Delete All App Data. This permanently removes all exported files, sync history, and cached data from your device. Copies of your medications, lab results, journal and check-ins in your iCloud are not removed by this. Data stored on VitaVault Cloud for OpenClaw sync is not removed by this; contact us to delete it.

Children

VitaVault is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
