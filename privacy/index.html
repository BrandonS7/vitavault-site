Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

The short version

Your health data is stored on your iPhone, and no account is needed. If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud and iCloud Sync is on (it is on by default), your medications, lab results, journal and check-ins also sync through your own iCloud account. When you use AI features or scans, only what that request needs is sent through our server to generate the answer, as described below. We don't keep your health data. The one exception is AI agent sync through VitaVault Cloud, which is off unless you turn it on (see AI Agent Sync below).

Data Collection

VitaVault reads health data from Apple HealthKit with your explicit permission. This data is:

  • Stored on your iPhone
  • Never uploaded or shared, except what an AI feature or scan sends when you use it (see AI Insights below), or what AI agent sync sends if you connect it
  • Never used for advertising or analytics
  • Only exported when you explicitly choose to export

AI Insights

AI features run only after you accept the consent prompt. Each request sends our Cloudflare Worker only what that feature needs: your question, and depending on the feature, a summary of your health numbers (such as steps, heart rate, and sleep hours), your medication list, or your lab results. Lab scan sends the text your iPhone read from the report, which can include anything printed on it. Label scan sends the label photo. The worker passes this to Anthropic Claude, with Google Gemini as a backup, to generate the answer, and does not keep it.

To apply monthly AI limits, each AI and scan request also carries a random install ID created by the app and, if you bought Premium or AI Insights, Apple's signed purchase record (product, transaction IDs, and purchase and expiry dates). Neither includes your name, email, or Apple Account. We keep only usage counters keyed to these IDs, and to your IP address for free users. The counters expire automatically.

If you don't use AI features, label scan, lab scan, or AI agent sync, no health data is sent to us or to any AI provider. Medications, lab results, journal and check-ins can still sync through your own iCloud (see iCloud Sync).

AI Agent Sync

AI agent sync is optional and off until you connect it in Settings (in the current app: Connect to OpenClaw) and confirm the consent prompt. It sends a daily summary of your Apple Health metrics (such as steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, distance, exercise minutes, flights climbed, weight and last night's sleep) to an AI agent you connect, so it can answer questions about them. It does not send your medications, lab results, journal or check-ins. Today the supported agent is OpenClaw. There are two ways to connect:

  • VitaVault Cloud: your data is stored on our server, which runs on Cloudflare, linked to a connection key unique to your device, and your agent reads it with that key. Each day's summary is kept for up to 365 days after it was last uploaded, and your most recent summary is kept until you ask us to delete it. VitaVault staff can access stored data to run and support the service. This is the only case where we store health data. It is not end-to-end encrypted: our server and the agent you connect can read it.
  • Direct webhook: your data goes straight from your iPhone to the web address and token you enter, without passing through our server.

Disconnect any time in Settings to stop syncing. Disconnecting stops new uploads but does not delete what is already stored. To have data already stored on VitaVault Cloud removed, contact us at the address below. Once your agent has your data, that agent's own privacy terms also apply.

Medication Data

Medication information you enter (names, dosages, reminders) is stored on your device, and in your own iCloud if iCloud Sync is on (see iCloud Sync below). Photos of medication labels are sent through our Cloudflare Worker to Anthropic Claude, with Google Gemini as a backup, to read the label, and the worker does not keep them.

iCloud Sync

If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud and the iCloud Sync switch in Settings is on (it is on by default), VitaVault syncs your medications, lab results, journal entries and daily check-ins through your own private iCloud account, so they can show up on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple stores this data in your iCloud, not on VitaVault's servers, and we can't see it. VitaVault does not sync your Apple Health readings or export files this way. You can turn this off at any time with that switch. Nothing syncs if your iPhone is not signed in to iCloud.

Exports

When you export your health data (JSON or CSV), the file is saved on your iPhone, inside the VitaVault app. If you tap Share Export, the file goes only where you send it. We never see it.

No Accounts

VitaVault does not require an account, email address, or any personal information to use. There is no sign-up process.

No Tracking

We do not use analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or any form of user behavior monitoring. We don't know who you are or how you use the app.

Third Parties

VitaVault does not sell or share your data for advertising. The only external communication is:

  • AI features and scans, sent through our server to Anthropic Claude, with Google Gemini as a backup, when you use them
  • AI agent sync, only if you connect it: VitaVault Cloud stores it on Cloudflare, and the agent you connect receives it
  • Apple iCloud, to sync your medications, lab results, journal and check-ins to your own iCloud account when your iPhone is signed in and iCloud Sync is on
  • Apple's App Store for in-app purchases

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time from Settings, Delete All App Data.

  • Delete from This iPhone: removes your labs, medications, journal, check-ins, photos, exports and cached data from this iPhone, turns iCloud sync off on this iPhone, and keeps your iCloud copy.
  • Also Delete from iCloud, then Delete Everywhere: VitaVault also deletes your labs, medications, journal and check-ins from your iCloud and from every device signed in to your Apple Account, and your other devices delete their photos and exports the next time they sync.

This can't be undone. Apple Health data is never affected. Data stored on VitaVault Cloud for AI agent sync is not removed by this; contact us to delete it.

Children

VitaVault is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes
