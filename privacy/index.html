Privacy Policy

VitaVault is built around a single principle: your health data belongs to you. We do not run cloud processing for your data and we do not collect your health exports.

What We Collect

We do not collect personal health data, export files, or account details. VitaVault works without account creation and without server-side storage.

How Data Is Processed

All health data processing happens on-device on your iPhone. Exported JSON/CSV files are saved to your Files app only when you choose to export them.

Sharing

We do not sell, broker, or share your health data. Data only leaves your device if you explicitly export and share it.
