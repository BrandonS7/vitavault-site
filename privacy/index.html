Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 17, 2026

The short version

VitaVault does not collect, store, or transmit your personal health data to any server. Everything stays on your device.

Data Collection

VitaVault reads health data from Apple HealthKit with your explicit permission. This data is:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Never uploaded to our servers or any third party
  • Never used for advertising or analytics
  • Only exported when you explicitly choose to export

AI Insights

When you use the AI Insights feature (free trial or subscription), an anonymized summary of your health metrics is sent to a secure AI endpoint (Google Gemini, via our Cloudflare Worker) to generate insights. This summary contains only numbers — steps, heart rate, sleep hours, etc. No name, no device ID, no location, nothing that identifies you. The AI provider does not store your data after processing.

If you never use the AI Insights, nothing ever leaves your phone.

Medication Data

Medication information you enter (names, dosages, reminders) is stored locally on your device. Photos of medication labels are processed through a secure cloud endpoint for text recognition only and are not retained after processing.

Exports

When you export your health data (JSON or CSV), the file is saved to your device's local storage via the iOS Files app. You control where that file goes — we never see it.

No Accounts

VitaVault does not require an account, email address, or any personal information to use. There is no sign-up process.

No Tracking

We do not use analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or any form of user behavior monitoring. We don't know who you are or how you use the app.

Third Parties

VitaVault does not share data with third parties. The only external communication is:

  • AI Insights requests (anonymized health summaries only, when you choose to use the feature)
  • Apple's App Store for in-app purchases

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time from Settings → Delete All App Data. This permanently removes all exported files, sync history, and cached data from your device.

Children

VitaVault is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions? Contact us through the support page.
