Wait — what's Apple Health data?

Your iPhone (and Apple Watch, if you have one) quietly tracks a ton about you — steps, sleep, heart rate, calories, blood oxygen, and way more. It all lives in the Apple Health app. But it's stuck there. You can look at it, but you can't really do anything with it.

VitaVault gets it out. You tap export, pick a format, and now you've got a file you can actually use — send it to your doctor, paste it into an AI, track it in a spreadsheet, whatever you want.

Here's what you can do

📤

Export in 3 formats

CSV opens in Excel or Google Sheets. JSON is for developers. AI-Ready is pre-formatted so you can paste it straight into ChatGPT or Claude and start asking questions.

🧠

Use any AI you want

Export your data, paste it into your favorite AI, and ask things like "What should I focus on this week?" or "Prepare a summary for my doctor." Your data, your AI, your choice.

✨

Or use our AI Coach

Don't want to copy and paste? Our built-in AI Coach reads your data and gives you actionable insights, doctor visit prep, and answers to your health questions — right in the app.

💊

Medication tracking

Point your camera at a pill bottle — AI reads the label and fills in everything. Set reminders so you never miss a dose. Free, no upgrade needed.

📊

Beautiful dashboard

See all your health metrics in one place — steps, sleep, heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, weight, calories, exercise. Charts, trends, everything. Always free.

🔒

Your data stays yours

Everything is processed on your phone. When you export, the file goes to your Files app — not our servers. No account needed. No sign-up. No tracking.

It takes about 30 seconds

1

Open the app, tap Allow

VitaVault asks for permission to read your Apple Health data. Tap allow, and that's it — your dashboard fills up immediately.

2

Browse or export

Check out your dashboard, or hit export. Pick CSV, JSON, or AI-Ready. Choose a time range. The file saves to your phone.

3

Do whatever you want with it

Paste it into ChatGPT. Email it to your doctor. Open it in Excel. Or skip all that and just ask our AI Coach. Up to you.
