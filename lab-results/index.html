Questions

Can VitaVault read a paper lab report?

Yes. Tap Scan Document and point your camera at the report. The text is read on your iPhone first, then the lab values are pulled out so you can review them before saving.

Can I upload a PDF from my patient portal?

Yes. Save the PDF to Files, then choose Upload PDFs. You can pick several PDFs at once and each one is processed separately.

Is my lab data sent anywhere?

Your saved lab results are stored on your iPhone, and no account is needed. To pull values out of a report or explain a result, VitaVault asks for your consent first, then sends only the text that request needs through our server to Anthropic Claude. Our server doesn't keep it. If you say no, VitaVault uses a less accurate extraction on your iPhone instead.

Does VitaVault diagnose anything?

No. Explanations are general health education only. Talk to your healthcare provider about what your results mean for you.

How much does it cost?

VitaVault is free to download and includes 10 AI queries in your first month. Premium is a one-time $4.99 purchase with 6 months of AI Insights included (50 a month, resets monthly). AI Insights is also available on its own for $2.99 a month.

VitaVault provides general health education only. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from your healthcare provider.
