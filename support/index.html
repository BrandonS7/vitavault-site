Common questions

How does pricing work? VitaVault is free to download, with a free first-month AI trial. Premium is a one-time $4.99 purchase — all features forever, plus a full year of AI Insights included. After that year, AI Insights can be continued with an optional monthly subscription.

Where is my health data stored? On your device. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone? Open the paywall (Settings → Premium) and tap "Restore Purchases" — it's tied to your Apple ID.

How do I connect my Apple Health data? See the in-app guide (Settings → Health Sources Guide) or the getting started guide.
